Gameplay

Feel the Force — again

Four new powers and higher levels for the classics. Nothing existing is changed; some powers are overpowered on purpose.

Telekinesis

Grab an NPC or an object and move it through space, The Force Unleashed style. Release mid-swing to throw.

setForceTelekinesis 3

Soar

Cling to walls, wall-run from mid-air, double jump after touching a wall and hang in the air for a few strikes.

setForceSoar 3

Deflect

Stop blaster bolts without a saber: freeze them, move while holding them, then send them back.

setforcedeflect 3

Chain Lightning

Lightning that jumps from enemy to enemy — two, five, or unlimited targets.

setForceChainLightning 3

Upgraded to level 4–5

Grip 5Three targets, longer range, ignores Absorb; combos with Lightning, Drain and attacks.
Push / Pull / Jump 5More range and power. Pull 5 disarms a weak saberist.
Saber Throw 4Throw both dual sabers together, or a saber staff.
Drain 4Strips Absorb, Protection and Heal 1–2, drains faster.
Heal 4Heals faster and raises max health to 120.

Physics

A world that pushes back

Spawn physical objects anywhere, or turn existing map entities physical. They react to the player, NPCs, Force Push / Pull, Grip, Telekinesis and saber hits.

  • Cloth — banners and curtains that react to bodies, the Force and sabers.
  • Movable objects — crates and barrels that knock NPCs down.
  • Map entities — make anything under your crosshair physical.
  • Deformables experimental — Verlet bodies and craters.
console
// where am I?
]get_position
// a cloth banner
]add_cloth 100 200 300 128 192 90 textures/kejim/metal
// crates in front of the player
]add_object imperial/crate_02 150
]add_object barrel 40
// the thing under the crosshair
]get_entity_id
]make_entity_physical 214 80

Modding

Per-map scripts

Bind config files to a map's load and unload. Give an item only on one level, retune the renderer for a single map, or furnish it with physical props — all from a .pk3.

  • Per-map items, weapons and Force powers
  • Per-map colour grading, fog and lighting
  • Per-map physics props and cloth
  • Per-map LUTs: maps/<map>.cube
maps_configs/t1_rail/give_force_deflect.cfg
setforcedeflect 1
maps_configs/t1_rail/give_force_deflect_unload.cfg
setforcedeflect 0

Always undo your changes in _unload so they don't leak into other maps.

Get started

Opt in, one cvar at a time

Install over your Jedi Academy copy, then enable what you want from the console. Most graphics features need r_hdr 1; some need a vid_restart.

  1. 1

    Get the build

    Build from source with CMake or grab a binary from the repository.

  2. 2

    Pick the renderer

    Select the rend2 renderer: cl_renderer rdsp-rend2 (SP) or rd-rend2 (MP).

  3. 3

    Turn things on

    Start with r_hdr 1, r_toneMapMode 2, r_aoMode 2, r_volumetricFog 2.
