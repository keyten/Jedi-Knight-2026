Jedi Academy 2026

A fork of OpenJK for Jedi Academy and Jedi Outcast. It adds rendering features to rend2, a few new Force powers and higher levels for existing ones, physical objects, and a way for mods to run scripts per map. Most of the work targets single player; some rendering changes apply to multiplayer as well.

Nothing changes by default. Every addition is behind a cvar or a console command, so the game, its saves and existing mods behave as before until you enable something.

Based on SomaZ's rend2-unified-wip branch of OpenJK.

Source on GitHub Rendering reference

Getting started

  1. 1

    Build the project with CMake from the source repository, the same way as OpenJK. You need an original copy of the game.

  2. 2

    Select the rend2 renderer: cl_renderer rdsp-rend2 in single player, cl_renderer rd-rend2 in multiplayer.

  3. 3

    Most rendering features require HDR: r_hdr 1. Some of them also need a vid_restart; the reference says which.

Graphics

Short descriptions with the main cvar for each feature. Requirements, all controls and debug views are listed in the rendering features reference.

Color and post-processing

Tone mapping

Legacy rend2 filmic, ACES fitted or AgX-like conversion from HDR to the display. r_toneMapDebug shows them side by side.

r_toneMapMode 0|1|2

Color grading

Applies a .cube LUT. A map can provide maps/<map>.cube; r_colorGradingLUT overrides it.

r_colorGrading 1

Linear lighting

Lighting is computed in linear space instead of sRGB.

r_linearLighting 1

Bloom and motion blur

HDR bloom and per-pixel velocity motion blur with a shutter-angle model.

r_motionBlur 1

Lighting and shadows

GTAO and contact shadows

Ground-truth ambient occlusion, with optional bent normals and specular occlusion, plus screen-space contact shadows (r_contactShadows).

r_aoMode 2

Forward+

Clustered dynamic lights instead of per-surface light lists.

r_forwardPlus 1

Screen-space GI

One bounce of indirect light from what is on screen, mainly for dynamic lights.

r_ssgi 1

Area lights

LTC area lights for lamps and panels, with automatic lamp detection, and line lights for sabers.

r_ltcAreaLights 1

Spot lights

Shadowed spot lights from effects, optionally with projected cookie textures.

r_spotLightCookies 1|2

Sun shadows

Cascaded sun shadows with caching and single-pass layered rendering, and adjustments for character shadows.

r_shadowLayered 1

Materials and reflections

Automatic PBR

Classifies legacy shaders by material and generates roughness variation for them.

r_autoPBR 1

Parallax occlusion

Adaptive POM with self-shadowing and silhouettes.

r_pomSilhouette 1

Skin

Subsurface scattering approximation for skin.

r_skinSSS 1

Emissive surfaces

Glowing surfaces as light emitters, compatible with the original glow.

r_pbrPhysical 1

Screen-space reflections

SSR combined with cubemaps; can include sabers and effects.

r_ssr 1

Volumetrics and sky

Volumetric fog

Froxel fog lit by the sun, dynamic and spot lights. Has a compute path on OpenGL 4.3.

r_volumetricFog 2

Local media

Fog volumes defined per map and volumetric smoke from effects, with emission, per-medium phase, self-shadowing, multiple scattering and colored extinction.

r_volumetricParticles 1

Atmosphere

Physically based sky and aerial perspective.

r_atmosphere 1

Clouds

Ray-marched volumetric clouds and cloud shadows (r_cloudShadows).

r_clouds 1

Water and weather

Water surface

PBR water with waves, tessellation, foam, flow, caustics, Snell's window and an underwater medium.

r_waterSurface 1

Underwater particles

Specks and bubbles under water.

r_waterParticles 1

Rain

Lit rain streaks and splashes where drops hit geometry.

r_rainSplashes 1

Wet surfaces

Surfaces exposed to rain get wet, with puddles, ripples and runoff.

r_weatherWetness 1

Lens water

Drops and water film on the camera lens.

r_rainLens 1

Foliage and loading

Grass cards

Surface sprite grass drawn as two- or three-card tufts.

r_grassCardMode 3

Wind

A shared wind field for grass and plants; r_leafFlutter adds leaf motion on models.

r_foliageWind 1

Interaction

Characters bend grass and plants they walk through.

Shader cache

Stores compiled shader programs on disk to shorten startup and vid_restart.

r_shaderProgramCache 1

Force powers

Existing powers keep their behavior at their original levels. New powers and levels are set from the console.

New powers

Telekinesis

Hold an NPC or an object and move it in space, similar to Grip in The Force Unleashed but without damage. Releasing it while moving throws it. Movement keys move the held target.

setForceTelekinesis 3

Soar

Stick to walls, start wall runs and flips in mid-air, jump again after touching a wall, and stay in the air for a few attacks.

setForceSoar 3

Deflect

Stop blaster bolts without a saber. Level 1 freezes a bolt, level 2 lets you move while doing it, level 3 sends it back.

setforcedeflect 3

Chain Lightning

Lightning that passes from one NPC to the next: up to 2, 5 or any number of targets. Bind it with +force_chain_lightning.

setForceChainLightning 3

Levels 4 and 5

Grip setForceGrip 5
Longer range, works on ungrippable NPCs, ignores Absorb, holds two or three NPCs. Combined with Lightning, Drain or an attack it becomes Chain Lightning, a pull into Drain, or an undeflectable pull and strike.
Push, Pull, Jump setForcePush 5
More range and strength. Pull 5 can take a saber from a weak opponent.
Saber Throw setSaberThrow 4
Throws both sabers in dual style, and the staff.
Drain setForceDrain 4
Cancels Absorb, Protection and Heal 1–2, ignores Push 1, drains faster.
Heal setForceHeal 4
Heals faster and raises maximum health to 120.

Physics

Objects can be added from the console. They react to the player, NPCs, Push, Pull, Grip, Telekinesis and saber hits. get_position prints your current coordinates.

There are also deformable objects (add_object_deform, make_box_deform) and commands that make all entities on a map physical. These are unfinished and unstable.

console
// add_cloth x y z width height angle [shader]
add_cloth 100 200 300 128 192 90 textures/kejim/metal

// add_object objectname mass
add_object imperial/crate_02 150
add_object models/map_objects/hoth/crate_snow.md3 120

// the entity under the crosshair
get_entity_id
make_entity_physical <entity_id> <mass>

Map scripts

A mod can run console configs when a map loads and unloads. This can be used to give an item on one map only, adjust rendering settings for a map, or place physical objects in it.

The unload config should undo everything the load config changed, so the settings don't carry over to other maps.

maps_configs/t1_rail/give_force_deflect.cfg
setforcedeflect 1
maps_configs/t1_rail/give_force_deflect_unload.cfg
setforcedeflect 0
