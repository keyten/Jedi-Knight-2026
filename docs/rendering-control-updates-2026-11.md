## Commands and diagnostics

- `r_materialClassificationDump` inspects semantic material results.
- `r_particleTest` creates controlled developer smoke fixtures; `profile-reset`, `profile-dump` and `info` inspect buffered timings and latched state. `r_particleProfile` controls recording.
- `r_lightReconInfo` now reports prepared fields and consumer diagnostics. Offline production, persistent cache preparation and reusable-field validation are linked from the [lighting research guide](rendering-features/lighting-tools.md).

These diagnostics do not turn a scene-level timing result into a general FPS claim. Existing benchmark reports retain their original source hashes, settings and limitations.
